# FAQ

## Is the Product Questions extension compatible with my custom store theme?

Our Product Questions extension is compatible with most Magento themes. However, some custom themes might need a small modification to display the Product Questions panel on the product page. You can find a tutorial on integrating Product Questions in a custom theme in [Integrating Product Questions in a Custom Theme](./09-integrating-product-questions-in-a-custom-theme.md). Check out [this tutorial](./10-integrating-product-questions-in-the-ultimo-theme.md) for specific instructions for Ultimo theme users!
